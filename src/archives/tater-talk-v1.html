FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our browser-based speech-to-text dictation app.

What browsers does Tater 🥔 Talk support?

⌄

Tater 🥔 Talk works best in Google Chrome and also supports Safari. It runs directly in your browser, with no downloads or extensions required.

Is Tater 🥔 Talk free?

⌄

Yes. Tater 🥔 Talk is free as in beer 🍺 You can optionally bring your own API keys to use premium speech models.

Which devices and platforms are supported?

⌄

Tater 🥔 Talk is cross‑platform: Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone, iPad, and Android. If your device has a modern browser and microphone, you're good to go.

How accurate is it? Do you store my audio?

⌄

Accuracy is around 99.5%, but depends on your microphone and speech. Speak clearly; like how a newscaster reads the news. Your browser sends your speech to Google, Apple, Microsoft, or Assembly AI to do the speech-to-text, similar to dictating a text message on your phone; see the Privacy Policy for details on data handling.

Can I use my own API keys?

⌄

Yes. Bring your own keys to unlock premium speech recognition models while keeping full control over your usage and costs.
