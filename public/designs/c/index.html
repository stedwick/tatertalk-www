House rules

I. What browsers does Tater Talk support?

It works best in Google Chrome and also supports Safari. It runs right in your browser, with no downloads or extensions.

II. Is Tater Talk free?

Yes. Tater Talk is free as in beer. You can bring your own API keys to use premium speech models.

III. Which devices and platforms are supported?

Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone, iPad, Android phones and Android tablets. If your device has a modern browser and a microphone, you're good to go.

IV. How accurate is it? Do you store my audio?

Accuracy depends on your microphone and how you speak. Speak clearly, like a newscaster reading the news. Your browser sends your speech to Google, Apple, Microsoft or AssemblyAI for speech-to-text, like dictating a text on your phone. See the privacy policy for how data is handled.

V. Can I use my own API keys?

Yes. Bring your own keys to use premium speech recognition models, with full control of your usage and costs.
