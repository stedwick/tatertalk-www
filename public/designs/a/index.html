Mission logbook

Questions from the ground

LOG 01 What browsers does Tater 🥔 Talk support?

Tater Talk works best in Google Chrome and also supports Safari. It runs right in your browser. No downloads, no extensions.

LOG 02 Is Tater 🥔 Talk free?

Yes. Tater Talk is free as in beer 🍺. You can bring your own API keys to use premium speech models if you like.

LOG 03 Which devices and platforms are supported?

Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android. If your device has a modern browser and a microphone, you're good to go.

LOG 04 How accurate is it? Do you store my audio?

Accuracy depends on your microphone and how you speak. Speak clearly, like a newscaster reading the news. Your browser sends your speech to Google, Apple, Microsoft or AssemblyAI to turn it into text, the same as dictating a text message on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.

LOG 05 Can I use my own API keys?

Yes. Bring your own keys to unlock premium speech recognition models, and keep full control of your usage and costs.
