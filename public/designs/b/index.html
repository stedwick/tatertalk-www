Questions people ask the potato

1 What browsers does Tater Talk support?

Tater Talk works best in Google Chrome and also supports Safari. It runs directly in your browser, with no downloads or extensions.

2 Is Tater Talk free?

Yes. Tater Talk is free as in beer 🍺 You can bring your own API keys to use premium speech models if you want.

3 Which devices and platforms are supported?

Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone, iPad, Android and Android tablets. If your device has a modern browser and a microphone, you are good to go.

4 How accurate is it? Do you store my audio?

Accuracy depends on your microphone and how you speak. Speak clearly, like a newscaster reads the news. Your browser sends your speech to Google, Apple, Microsoft or AssemblyAI to turn it into text, like dictating a text message on your phone. See the privacy policy for how data is handled.

5 Can I use my own API keys?

Yes. Bring your own keys to unlock premium speech models, and keep full control of your usage and costs.
